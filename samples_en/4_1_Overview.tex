\chapter{Results}


This chapter presents the results of the experimental evaluation of the selected
code translation models. The performance of the base and fine-tuned variants of
starcoder2-3b and Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct was analyzed using both similarity-based and
execution-based metrics.

Table~\ref{tab:relative_improvement} summarizes the relative improvements
observed after fine-tuning across different evaluation metrics. For starcoder2-3b, the average BLEU score increased by 54.6\%, while the number
of accepted solutions increased by 141.8\%. At the same time, the number of
compilation errors decreased by 26.4\%. For Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct, the average BLEU score increased by 22.1\%, and
the number of accepted solutions increased by 66.3\%. Compilation errors also
decreased by 32.4\%.
