\section{BLEU Score Results}

BLEU score was used to measure the similarity between the generated C++
translations and the reference outputs. For each model, the BLEU score was
calculated for every translated sample and then averaged across the entire
evaluation dataset. Table~\ref{tab:bleu_results} presents the BLEU score statistics for the evalated models.



For starcoder2-3b , the average BLEU score increased from 0.122 for the base model
to 0.189 for the fine-tuned model. This corresponds to an improvement of
approximately 54.6\%. In addition, the maximum BLEU score increased from 0.922
to 0.981, indicating that the fine-tuned model was able to produce translations
that were much closer to the reference solutions in the best-performing cases.

Similarly, the Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct model also benefited from fine-tuning. The average BLEU
score increased from 0.178 for the base model to 0.217 for the fine-tuned
model, which corresponds to an improvement of approximately 22.1\%. The maximum
BLEU score also increased substantially, from 0.780 to 0.980.

The minimum BLEU scores remained very low for all models, with values close to
zero in each case. This indicates that for some samples the generated outputs
have very low similarity to the reference solutions.

The median BLEU scores differ from the average values across all models. For
StarCoder2-3b, the median decreased from 0.070 for the base model to 0.063 after
fine-tuning. For Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct, the median decreased from 0.120 to
0.043 after fine-tuning.

The standard deviation values show noticeable variation in BLEU scores across
samples. For StarCoder2-3b, the standard deviation increased from 0.165 to 0.256
after fine-tuning. Similarly, for Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct, the standard
deviation increased from 0.156 to 0.244.
